# Project Context

## Purpose

**Die Zauberschmiede** is a ready-made Minecraft Bedrock Survival world for a child and parent. The first version should be playable on an iPhone and an Amazon Kids tablet and quick for the parent to install and verify from a Mac.

## People

- **Child**: designs the spawn area and proposes the short magical wording in the guidebook.
- **Parent**: prepares the project on a Mac, installs it on the devices, and tests the world and recipes.
- **Players**: the parent and child playing together, usually over home Wi-Fi.

## Ubiquitous language

- **Die Zauberschmiede**: the project and Bedrock world name.
- **Handbuch der Zauberschmiede**: the readable, primarily German guidebook placed in the starter chest.
- **Starter chest**: the chest near spawn containing the guidebook and enough materials to try every custom recipe.
- **Custom recipe**: one of the three deliberately easy crafting conversions defined for the first playable version.
- **First playable version**: a complete, testable world that the parent can prepare in roughly 45–60 minutes.

## Product constraints

- Use Minecraft Bedrock Edition behavior that works without experimental creator features.
- Deliver the guidebook through the starter chest, not automatic player inventory delivery.
- Preserve Survival play while making the custom recipes easy to discover and test.
- Keep guidebook instructions primarily in German, with exact recipes and short magical flavor sentences.
